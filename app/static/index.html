RESEARCH PRINCIPLE

Measure uncertainty.
Never manufacture confidence.

BhashaBridge explores why word error rate alone may miss meaning-critical errors. The included evaluator reports exact-match metrics on illustrative examples; real-world reliability requires evaluation on consented, recorded speech and downstream action outcomes.
